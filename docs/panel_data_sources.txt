The month names are spelled out in the file for temperature, precipitation, and evapotranspiration. Wind speed uses the abbreviations above. "PERCIPITATION" and "Centigrate" are the spellings in the header.

Required fields, all present:
Rice yield: RICE YIELD (Kg per ha). Also rice area and rice production, both in thousands, so production divided by area is tonnes per hectare. The stored yield matches that conversion within about 0.3 kg per hectare on the median, which is rounding. The study uses the stored yield column.
Rainfall: the twelve monthly precipitation columns, millimeters.
Year: Year. District id: Dist Code.

Preferred irrigation field, present but not rice-specific: GROSS IRRIGATED AREA (1000 ha). There is no rice-irrigated-area column.

Each of June, July, August, September precipitation, and the Rainy JUN-SEP column: 148 blank. No -1.
Rice area above zero: 11,970 rows. Rice yield above zero: 11,969 rows.
Rows with rice area and yield above zero and all four monsoon months present and at least zero: 11,805 rows, 531 districts.
Of those, rows that also have a usable irrigation share: 10,139 rows, 523 districts. The share exceeds 1 in 66 of them and exceeds 2 in 3. They are kept.
Years up to 2007 in that irrigation sample: 6,923 rows, 493 districts. 425 districts have at least 8 such early years. Years from 2008 on: 3,216 rows, 467 districts.

The Rainy JUN-SEP column is not the sum of June, July, August, and September. On 12,655 rows where both exist, the median absolute gap is 639 mm. The study sums the four months and does not use the rainy-season column.
